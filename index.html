About Our Project

Listening to patients is an important step towards improvement.

This Community Engagement Project focuses on understanding the importance of hospital facilities and patient care through feedback. It aims to identify areas where patients may experience difficulties and how their suggestions can support better healthcare services.

Project Objectives

What this project aims to understand

🏥

Evaluate Facilities

Understand perceptions of cleanliness, waiting areas, and basic hospital amenities.

💙

Understand Patient Care

Explore patient experiences with staff behaviour, communication, and support.

📈

Identify Improvements

Use feedback themes to suggest ways to improve the patient experience.

Why Is Patient Feedback Needed?

Patient feedback helps healthcare providers understand service quality from the patient's perspective. It can reveal concerns about waiting time, cleanliness, staff communication, accessibility, and comfort. Reviewing this feedback can help hospitals prioritise improvements and build trust with patients.

Methodology

A simple and organised approach to collecting feedback

  1. Prepare a questionnaire: Create clear questions about facilities and patient care.
  2. Seek permission: Obtain approval from the hospital before collecting responses.
  3. Explain the purpose: Inform participants about the educational project and obtain voluntary consent.
  4. Collect feedback: Use an anonymous questionnaire without requesting unnecessary personal or medical information.
  5. Review responses: Summarise the feedback and identify recurring suggestions.
  6. Recommend improvements: Prepare practical suggestions based on the responses actually collected.

Conclusion

Patient feedback is a valuable way to understand experiences with hospital facilities and care. A well-planned, respectful feedback process can help identify opportunities for improvement, encourage better communication, and support patient-centred services. Actual findings and recommendations should be based on responses collected with appropriate permission and consent.
